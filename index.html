Cutting back on soda and malt

The plan keeps soda or malt to two treat drinks a week. Malt drinks taste less “fizzy-drink” than soda, but they are usually just as sugary, so they count as a treat too.

Instead ofTry
Soda with lunchFlavored sparkling water with no sugar. It keeps the fizz.
Malt with rice dishes or swallowZobo made lightly sweet, or cold unsweetened iced tea
Juice all dayJuice mixed half and half with water, or water with lemon or cucumber
A soda at a fast food stopOrder water or unsweetened tea, and keep the soda for a treat day
